Voice roles of the 26 voice actor guests

Every voice credit found for the 26 voice actors on GalaxyCon St. Louis's guest list, well beyond the headline characters on their guest cards. Pick a guest, filter by type, or search a character to see who voiced it. On-camera acting roles are left out, so actors who mostly work on camera, like Sam Witwer, Janet Varney and Brandon Rogers, show only their voice work.

Sources: anime credits come from the Anime News Network encyclopedia (English dub cast lists, one entry per season or film), merged with each actor's Wikipedia filmography, which also supplies most video game, Western animation and film credits. Entries found in both sources were merged by title. Alex Rochon and Zelda Khan Black have neither, so their credits (and a few extra ones for Amanda Hufford and Michaela Laws) come from the actors' own sites and resumés and the Glitch Productions, ZAMination and Amazing Digital Circus fan wikis. Compiled September 29, 2026 from the GalaxyCon St. Louis voice guests page. Some small or uncredited roles may still be missing, and entries with no character listed are ones where the source doesn't name the role. Alejandro Saab is also credited as Cy Yu; Mike Pollock's Wikipedia entry is Mike Pollock (voice actor); Matthew Wood is the Lucasfilm sound editor who voices General Grievous and the battle droids.
