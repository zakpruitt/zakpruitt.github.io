Voice roles of the 13 voice actor guests

Every voice credit found for the voice actors on Collect-A-Con's Chicago 2 special guests page, well beyond the few characters on their guest cards. Pick a guest, filter by type, or search a character to see who voiced it. On-camera acting roles are left out. The other two guests, Steve Aoki and Goat Breakers, aren't voice actors.

Sources: anime credits come from the Anime News Network encyclopedia (English dub cast lists, one entry per season or film), merged with each actor's Wikipedia filmography, which also supplies most video game, Western animation and film credits. Entries found in both sources were merged by title. Compiled September 26, 2026 from the Chicago 2 special guests page. Some small or uncredited roles may still be missing, and entries with no character listed are ones where the source doesn't name the role. Cristina Vee is also credited as Cristina Valenzuela.
